Things you can say


        
Flying


takeoff()
land()
hover(2)
wait(1)


        
Moving


fly("forward", 1, 40)
turn("right", 90)

        

          fly direction, seconds, speed

          forward · backward · left

          right · up · down

          speed 5–100
        

        

          turn "left" or "right",

          1–180 degrees
        


        
Tricks


flip("front")
flip_times("front", 3, 1.2)

        

          front · back · left · right

          flip_times skips the settle
          between flips — climb first.
          2–5 flips, gap 0.2–3s.
        


        
Safety


emergency()

        

          Cuts the motors. The drone
          falls. Only for a fly-away.
        


        
Showing things


print("hello", 1 + 2)

        

          Appears on your teacher's screen.
